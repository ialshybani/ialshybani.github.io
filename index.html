01 / PROFILE

About me

I am a computational scientist and mechanical engineering researcher working at the intersection of fluid mechanics, combustion, and large-scale scientific computing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Michigan State University, where I investigated turbulence–shock–combustion interactions using direct numerical simulation. My research addresses compressibility effects, scalar mixing, chemical reaction, and the evolution of turbulent reacting flows.

At Purdue University's Rosen Center for Advanced Computing, I support researchers using advanced HPC systems, including parallel scientific applications, CFD workflows, performance troubleshooting, and technical training.
